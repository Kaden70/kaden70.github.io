Kaden’s Plant Defense Lab

I want to engineer plants to their fullest potential, so my goal is to develop real life plants featured in the hit game "Plants vs. Zombies." You will be able to grow these plants and they can keep you company and even defend your property from robbers, zombies, or anything else.

Plants

Peashooter

A modified pea plant that shoots enlarged peas. Good against any threat.

Fume Shroom

A mushroom that sprays fumes. Even though it's not a plant, they will defend your home at night.

Cattail

A plant that lives in pools and shoots spikes. If you have a pool or lake then these will work great in there.

Sunflower

Sunflowers generate sun to plant other plants. They can keep you company but they don't defend.
